# MediCore – Privacy & legal

Last updated: September 2026

## Overview

MediCore stores your health data on your own devices. There is no MediCore account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. We do not sell or share your data. Also documents you add yourself (PDF or photos), with title, date and flag.

## What is stored

Medications, doses, intake logs, measurements (blood pressure, glucose, weight, pulse, oxygen, temperature, lab values), your Medical ID (name, birth date, blood type, allergies, conditions, doctor, emergency contact, photo) and your settings. All of it is saved on your device first, so the app works offline.

## iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and leave sync on, your data is stored in your private iCloud (CloudKit) container and synced between your own devices. Only you can access it; we have no access. You can turn sync off in Settings.

## Apple Health

With your permission MediCore reads and writes blood pressure, glucose, weight, heart rate, oxygen saturation and body temperature, and reads your medication list. You choose each permission in iOS and can withdraw it any time in Settings > Health. Health data is never used for advertising or passed to third parties.

## Drug search

When you search for a medication online, only the search term is sent to the public databases openFDA (US FDA) and RxNorm (US National Library of Medicine). No name, no health data and no device identifier is sent. Offline search stays on your device.

## Notifications

Reminders are created on your device. They do not pass through any server.

## Apple Watch and widgets

Your dose schedule is shared between iPhone, Apple Watch and widgets through Apple's system services on your own devices.

## Reports and sharing

PDF reports are created on your device. They leave the app only when you share or print them yourself; then the privacy rules of the receiving app or person apply.

## Your rights

You can view, edit or delete every entry in the app. Settings > Delete all data removes everything from the device and, with sync on, from your iCloud copy. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability (GDPR, KVKK). Since we hold no data about you, requests concern only your own devices and iCloud.

## Children

MediCore is not aimed at children under 13. Parents may manage medications for a child.

## Not medical advice

MediCore is a tracking and reminder aid. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Drug information, ratings and explanations are general and may be incomplete or out of date. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions and check the leaflet. In an emergency call your local emergency number. Do not rely on reminders alone for critical medication.
